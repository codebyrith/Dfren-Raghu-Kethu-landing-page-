The Karmic Shift of Raghu & Kethu (Peyarchi)

✦ ॐ ✦

Unlike physical planets, Raghu and Kethu are mathematical celestial intersection nodes (Chhaya Grahas) that control our deepest karmic desires and spiritual liberation.

🐉

Lord Raghu: The North Node (Head of the Dragon)

Desire • Material Ambition • Innovation • Sudden Elevation

In Vedic Jyotish, Raghu symbolizes intense hunger for accomplishment, foreign adventures, cutting-edge technology, and sudden unexpected fortune. When positioned favorably in 3rd, 6th, 10th, or 11th houses from your Janma Rasi, Raghu can bestow unprecedented fame, executive leadership, and windfall gains.

⚡ Positive Manifestations: International travel, viral recognition, rapid promotions, digital ventures.
⚠️ Shadow Precautions: Unrealistic expectations, illusionary shortcuts, impulsive financial speculation.
🚩

Lord Kethu: The South Node (Tail of the Dragon)

Moksha • Intuition • Detachment • Spiritual Wisdom

Kethu represents our accumulated past-life spiritual merit (Poorva Punya), deep intuition, mastery of subtle energies, and ultimate liberation. Kethu cuts through vanity to awaken truth. Placed in auspicious houses (like 3rd, 6th, or 12th), Kethu crushes rivals, dissolves chronic ailments, and awakens profound wisdom.

🕉️ Positive Manifestations: Heightened intuition, victory over competition, research mastery, spiritual calm.
⚠️ Shadow Precautions: Emotional detachment from worldly duties, mental confusion, ungrounded decisions.
